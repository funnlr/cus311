Exam Guidelines

SECTION A — COMPULSORY (15 marks)
  • Explain the key issue in the dispute
  • Critique within the story of African customary law
  • Cite ONLY relevant prescribed readings
  • Include at least 2 relevant quotations with page numbers
  • 400–500 words (up to 550 allowed)
SECTION B — FIVE QUESTIONS (7 marks each)
  • No referencing required
  • Base answers on lectures and prescribed readings
  • Support opinions with emphasis on lectures
  • For statements: explain why you agree/disagree
  • Use only class discussions where specified
Key Requirements
  • Use your own ideas and writing style
  • Questions are designed to test your thinking
  • Reference only when relevant (not for volume)
  • Books and journal articles: full citations
  • Laws and judgements: short citations only
